# Chronicle Vault

Create a premium, cinematic blogging website called THE HIDDEN CHRONICLE.

BRAND

THE HIDDEN CHRONICLE

Tagline:

“Stories history didn’t finish telling.”

The website is a dedicated blog about historical mysteries, forgotten events, unexplained discoveries, lost artifacts, mysterious disappearances, ancient puzzles, historical codes, and unanswered questions from the past.

This should feel like a real independent editorial publication, not a school project, portfolio, or generic AI-generated blog.

1. OVERALL VISUAL DESIGN

Create a sophisticated, cinematic, mysterious and timeless aesthetic.

Colors

Deep charcoal / near-black background

Warm ivory text

Very subtle antique-gold accents

Muted sepia tones for imagery

Typography

Use an elegant high-contrast serif font for large headlines and article titles.

Use a clean modern sans-serif font for navigation, metadata, buttons and body text.

Visual style

Think:

Luxury historical magazine × investigative documentary × archival journal

Use:

Large cinematic historical photography

Old manuscripts

Archival documents

Ancient artifacts

Faded photographs

Maps

Subtle paper texture

Very subtle film grain

Thin divider lines

Large amounts of negative space

Smooth scrolling

Very subtle hover animations

Elegant transitions

Do NOT make the website overly filled.

Avoid:

Bright colors

Excessive gold

Too many cards

Too many icons

Huge amounts of text on the homepage

Cartoonish visuals

Fantasy-looking graphics

Excessive animations

Clutter

The overall feeling should be dark, intelligent, expensive, calm and mysterious.

2. HEADER / NAVIGATION

Create a clean, minimal header.

Left:

THE HIDDEN CHRONICLE

Navigation:

HOME | STORIES | CATEGORIES | ABOUT

Right:

SEARCH icon

Keep the header elegant and spacious.

3. HOMEPAGE

The homepage should primarily function as the front page of a historical-mystery magazine.

HERO SECTION

Use one large cinematic historical image with a subtle dark overlay.

Display:

THE HIDDEN CHRONICLE

Stories history didn’t finish telling.

Small description:

Forgotten evidence. Lost stories. Questions that survived centuries.

Button:

EXPLORE THE STORIES →

Keep this section spacious and dramatic.

4. FEATURED STORY

Create one large featured article rather than filling the page with many cards.

Small label:

FEATURED INVESTIGATION

Large title:

THE BOOK THAT REFUSES TO SPEAK

Subtitle:

The Voynich Manuscript

Add a short introduction explaining that the manuscript contains an unknown writing system, strange illustrations and remains the subject of continuing research and debate.

Place a large atmospheric manuscript image beside or behind the content.

Button:

READ THE INVESTIGATION →

5. LATEST STORIES

Create a clean editorial blog grid with only three featured articles visible at once.

Each article should contain:

Large image

Category

Article title

Short excerpt

Custom publication date

Reading time

View count

READ STORY →

Example articles:

THE COLONY THAT VANISHED

The Lost Colony of Roanoke

THE PRISONER WITHOUT A NAME

The Man in the Iron Mask

THE MACHINE BEFORE ITS TIME

The Antikythera Mechanism

Allow the admin to continuously add unlimited new articles.

6. INDIVIDUAL BLOG ARTICLE PAGE

This is the most important part of the website.

Every article should open on a beautiful, distraction-free reading page.

At the top show:

CATEGORY · CUSTOM DATE · READING TIME

Then a large serif headline.

Example:

THE BOOK THAT REFUSES TO SPEAK

The Voynich Manuscript

Then display a large cinematic hero image.

Below the image, include the article interaction bar:

♡ LIKE 💬 COMMENT 👁 VIEWS ↗ SHARE

Example:

♡ 248 Likes · 💬 36 Comments · 👁 2.4K Views

These should be functional and dynamically update.

7. ARTICLE STRUCTURE

Make the article reading experience elegant and easy to read.

Articles can contain sections such as:

THE DISCOVERY

Introduce the mystery.

WHAT WE KNOW

Present established historical evidence.

THE QUESTIONS

Explain what remains uncertain.

THEORIES

Present different theories clearly and distinguish theories from established facts.

THE EVIDENCE

Discuss important discoveries, documents or archaeological evidence.

WHAT REMAINS UNSOLVED

End with the unanswered questions.

Allow articles to include:

Images

Captions

Pull quotes

Timelines

Historical documents

Maps

Videos where appropriate

Related links

Keep the content width comfortable for reading with generous spacing.

8. LIKE SYSTEM

Every article must have a functional Like button.

Display:

♡ LIKE

When clicked:

♥ LIKED

Show the total number of likes.

The like count should update dynamically and persist.

9. COMMENTS

Every article must have a functional comment section.

Readers should be able to:

Write comments

Post comments

View other comments

Reply to comments

Like comments

Display the total number of comments.

Example:

💬 36 COMMENTS

Use a clean editorial comment design that does not clutter the page.

If authentication is required, allow users to create/sign into an account before posting while still allowing anyone to read the articles.

10. VIEW COUNTER

Every article should have a working view counter.

Display:

👁 2.4K VIEWS

Views should update dynamically when an article is viewed.

Show the view count on:

Article page

Homepage article cards

Category pages

11. FOLLOW SYSTEM

Add a FOLLOW button for the publication/author.

Example:

+ FOLLOW

After clicking:

✓ FOLLOWING

Allow readers to follow the blog and receive updates when new articles are published.

The follower count can also be displayed subtly.

12. SHARE

Every article should have a working SHARE button.

Allow readers to share the article through available social platforms or copy the article link.

Keep the share controls minimal.

13. CUSTOMIZABLE ARTICLE DATE

This is very important.

The website admin must manually control the publication date of every article.

When creating or editing an article, provide an admin field:

DATE PUBLISHED

The admin should be able to select or enter any date.

Do NOT automatically replace the chosen date with the upload date.

For example, if an article is uploaded today but the admin wants the article to display:

14 OCTOBER 2026

the website should display exactly that date.

The admin must also be able to change the date later without changing the article URL or content.

Use an elegant display format:

14 OCTOBER 2026

The custom date must appear consistently on:

Homepage

Article cards

Individual article pages

Category pages

Search results

Featured stories

14. CATEGORIES

Create a dedicated CATEGORIES page.

Use these categories:

LOST

Lost civilizations, settlements and forgotten worlds.

HIDDEN

Secret documents, artifacts and discoveries.

UNSOLVED

Disappearances and unexplained historical events.

MISSING

Lost treasures and vanished objects.

DECIPHERED

Ancient manuscripts, codes and mysterious writings.

ANCIENT

Mysteries surrounding ancient civilizations and discoveries.

Each category should open a clean page containing relevant blog posts.

15. SEARCH

Create a functional search system.

Readers should be able to search articles by:

Title

Keyword

Category

Topic

Show clean search results with article image, title, excerpt, date and views.

16. RELATED STORIES

At the end of every article, show:

RELATED STORIES

Display three relevant articles based on category or topic.

Each should have:

Image
Category
Title
Date
Views
READ STORY →

17. ARTICLE END

After the article:

Did this mystery leave you with questions?

Then display:

♡ LIKE · 💬 COMMENT · ↗ SHARE

Followed by related stories.

18. NEWSLETTER

Add a subtle newsletter section near the bottom of the homepage.

Title:

ENTER THE ARCHIVE

Text:

“Occasional stories from the forgotten corners of history.”

Button:

SUBSCRIBE →

Keep it minimal and elegant.

19. FOOTER

Minimal footer.

THE HIDDEN CHRONICLE

Forgotten stories. Unanswered questions.

Navigation:

Stories · Categories · About · Contact

Add subtle social icons.

20. ADMIN / CMS

Create an easy-to-use admin/content management system where I can:

Create new articles

Edit articles

Delete articles

Upload article images

Choose categories

Add tags

Set a custom publication date

Change the publication date later

Add excerpts

Add featured articles

See likes

See comments

See views

Manage comments

See followers

Manage published/draft articles

The blog must support unlimited future articles.

21. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Make the entire website responsive for:

Desktop

Laptop

Tablet

Mobile

On mobile, maintain the same premium editorial aesthetic rather than simply shrinking the desktop layout.

FINAL DESIGN DIRECTION

The final website should feel like:

A mysterious independent magazine documenting the unanswered stories of history.

Prioritize beautiful typography, cinematic imagery, whitespace and storytelling.

Do not overcrowd the homepage.

The website should make visitors want to click an article and spend time reading it.

Premium. Minimal. Cinematic. Mysterious. Editorial.

The main purpose is BLOGGING, so the individual article pages, CMS, reading experience, comments, likes, views, follows and customizable publication dates are more important than decorative homepage elements.
